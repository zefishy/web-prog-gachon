Week 9_1 — Exercise 1

Fix a <div> used as a button into a real <button> so that keyboard users can Tab to it and screen readers announce it correctly.

Exercise 1 — Div → Button

The original page used a <div onclick="..."> as a button. That is invisible to keyboard users and screen readers. The fix: replace it with a <button type="button"> so it is focusable, activatable with Enter/Space, and announced with its accessible name.

Try it

Press Tab to reach the button, then Enter or Space to activate it.

What changed — before vs after 
<div class="btn" onclick="alert('Clicked!')">Submit form</div>

→ becomes:

<button type="button" class="btn" onclick="alert('Clicked!')">Submit form</button>
