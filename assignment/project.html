A Better Website for Gachon Wind Baseball Club

Team 16 — Web Programming Club Project

Gachon University

The club's information is scattered across a CCA listing with a broken homepage link and an Instagram account that rarely updates. This page collects our proposal for one reliable home for Gachon Wind: the problems, the website we'll build, the team behind it, and how we'll deliver it.

About the project

Finding Gachon Wind online today ends in four dead ends: unclear joining instructions, missing photos, videos, and practice timetables, a broken homepage link on the university's CCA page, and no proper contacts. The team's Instagram, @GCU_WIND, has real photos but limited updates, and nowhere can students find a schedule, a sign-up form, or clear contact details. International students feel it most.

Our solution is one website for the club that always works. Club information, photos, and videos live in one place; a schedule lists every future baseball game; an online sign-up form and clear contacts replace the dead ends; and every page is available in English, Korean, Vietnamese, and Japanese.

Unlike the static CCA page, the site stays current; unlike Instagram, joining takes a few clicks; and a mobile-first, four-language layout makes it truly accessible for every Gachon student.

Key features

  • Club profile, activities, and upcoming events in one place
  • A schedule of every future baseball game
  • Sign-up form, joining steps, and contact details
  • Mobile-friendly, interactive photo and video gallery with a language switch

We'll deliver it in four phases: plan and design, develop, test and feedback on phones, browsers, and with Wind members and students, then launch and hand the site to the club to keep updated.
