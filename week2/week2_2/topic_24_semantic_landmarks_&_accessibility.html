Semantic landmarks & accessibility

Semantics are an accessibility feature, not a style preference.

Screen reader users jump between landmarks instead of reading from the top. The elements on this page provide them automatically.

ElementLandmark
headerbanner
navnavigation
mainmain
asidecomplementary
footercontentinfo

Repeated landmarks of the same kind need labels to be distinguishable.

The markup

<header>banner</header>
<nav>navigation</nav>
<main>main</main>
<aside>complementary</aside>
<footer>contentinfo</footer>
