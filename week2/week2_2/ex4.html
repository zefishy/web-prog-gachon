Frequently Asked Questions

What is HTML?

HTML stands for HyperText Markup Language. It is the standard language for creating web pages and describes the structure of content.

What is the difference between <section> and <article>?

A section groups related content that needs its surrounding context. An article is fully self-contained and could be published independently.

Why is the alt attribute important?

The alt attribute provides alternative text for images. Screen readers read it to visually impaired users, and it displays when the image fails to load.

What does DOCTYPE do?

The DOCTYPE declaration tells the browser which version of HTML the document uses. <!DOCTYPE html> triggers standards mode for consistent rendering across all browsers.
