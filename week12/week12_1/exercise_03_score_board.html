Week 12_1 · Exercise 3

Score board

Two players, one owner. The board holds both scores; each player gets a number and two callbacks, and the total is computed from the scores rather than stored.

Nothing is duplicated: the players display what the board owns, and the total is recalculated on every render instead of being kept as a third piece of state.
