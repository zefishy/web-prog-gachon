Step 5 · ShoppingCart component

The finished build: one owner of the cart state, three presentational components, and every number on screen derived from that single array.

What the component tree looks like

<ShoppingCart>              ← owns the cart state
  ├── <ProductList>          ← receives products + onAdd
  │     └── <ProductCard>    ← one product, one Add button
  └── <CartPanel>            ← receives cart + onRemove + onQuantity
        └── <CartRow>        ← one line, its own − / + buttons
  • At least 5 products with name, price and image
  • Add and remove items without mutating the array
  • A cart panel whose count and total are derived
  • Quantity controls that drop a line back to zero
  • One component owning the state, the rest receiving props
